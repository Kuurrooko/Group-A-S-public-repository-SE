## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, athletic, and systematic interface tailored for modern tennis clubs, regional sports associations, and multi-surface racquets facilities. It fuses the structural rigor of European civic club software with the dynamic, tactile energy of contemporary competitive tennis.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Athletic Precision:** Crisp lines, strict time-block alignments, and zero visual ambiguity under high sun or hurried mobile check-ins.
- **Dynamic Energy:** High-octane cadmium orange accents paired with expansive clean white planes, conveying kinetic movement, ball velocity, and modern sportsmanship.
- **Reliable Authority:** Uncompromising clarity for member verification, match logs, payment ledgers, and court supervisor maintenance logs.

### Design Movement: Modern Swiss-Athletic
The aesthetic merges International Typographic Style (structured mathematical grids, clear hierarchy, architectural tabular layouts) with tactile sports minimalism. Translucent glass treatments are discarded in favor of pure optical contrast: bright optic whites, sharp baseline rhythms, functional surface indicators (clay terracotta, grass meadow, hardcourt azure), and decisive state elevations.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high daytime legibility (R-01 compliance) and instantaneous environmental recognition across court types and booking states.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Accent (`#EA580C` / `#FF6B00`):** Electric clay-court orange. Reserved exclusively for actionable focal points: primary booking CTAs, active timeline runners, active navigation indicators, and urgent role toggles. Must maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast against dark slate surfaces or use shaded variants on white.
- **Canvas & Layering (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`):** Pure white dominates content containers, booking sheets, and interactive card faces, framed by cool tinted neutral slates (`#F1F5F9` and `#E2E8F0`) for visual stratification.
- **Neutral Typography (`#0F172A` / `#334155`):** Deep ink slate delivering uncompromising legibility against white and light stone backgrounds, preventing color vibration.

### Contextual Surface Tones (Specialized Sports Semantics)
- **Clay / Sandplatz (`#C2410C`):** Terracotta rust used strictly for clay court badges, maintenance warning banners, and court-type chips.
- **Hardcourt / Plexicushion (`#0284C7`):** Secondary acrylic cyan for outdoor and indoor hardcourt categorizations.
- **Grass / Rasenplatz (`#15803D`):** Tertiary court green dedicated to natural and synthetic turf schedules.
- **Carpet / Indoor Halle (`#B45309`):** Warm amber for indoor granulate and wooden flooring schedules.

### Functional States
- **Available Slot:** Pure `#FFFFFF` fill with a 1px `#CBD5E1` border and an subtle hover wash of `#FFF7ED`.
- **Occupied / Confirmed:** `#F1F5F9` fill with subtle diagonal hatch or `#64748B` metadata tags.
- **Active Hold / In-Cart:** `#FFF7ED` fill with a solid 2px `#EA580C` border.
- **Maintenance / Platzwart Block:** Solid `#FEF2F2` base with `#DC2626` warning borders and badges.

## Typography

The typographic system provides instant scan-readiness, balancing geometric athletic impact with high-density tabular utility.

### Hierarchy & Role Allocation
- **Display & Section Headers (`Space Grotesk`):** Technical, angular grotesque providing structural framing for club names, court numbers (e.g., "COURT 03 - CENTER"), score tickers, and booking modal titles.
- **Body & Controls (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Modern, open humanist grotesque built for rapid parsing of player rosters, booking guidelines, billing terms, and dialog instructions.
- **Data, Timers & Verification (`JetBrains Mono`):** Monospaced precision for hour increments (`08:00 - 09:30`), digital PIN locks, court RFID tokens, and Platzwart audit logs.

### Numerical Tabular Alignments
All calendar cells, time matrices, and membership balances must enforce tabular numbers (`tnum` OpenType feature) to guarantee columnar alignment regardless of shifting widths in time increments or currencies.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a 4px base increment structured within an architectural dashboard shell.

### Grid Matrix & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Fluid 12-column grid. Left pinned navigation rail (80px collapsed / 240px expanded), central booking matrix spanning remaining columns with a dedicated 360px sticky sidebar for active court selections, member loyalty balance, and weather status.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Court scheduling headers scroll horizontally with frozen time-stamps on the vertical axis.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column compact layout. Court selection shifts to tabbed pill switchers or segmented day ribbons; time slots span full device width with sticky bottom booking trays.

### Booking Matrix Spacing Rhythm
- **Court Columns:** Minimum width of 160px per court column on multi-court views to prevent player tag truncation.
- **Time Block Increments:** 30-minute default height pegged to 48px, offering a tap-friendly touch target for thumb booking on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp architectural boundaries rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing a light, clean, and sunny athletic feel.

### Surface Tiers & Boundaries
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational backdrop for the entire application.
- **Primary Worksurface (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF`. High-contrast containment cards, calendar matrices, and filter bars framed by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Hover & Active Focus (Level 2):** Subtle kinetic elevation using an ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Border transitions sharply from slate to `#EA580C`.
- **Flyouts, Dropdowns & Modals (Level 3):** Crisp elevation: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with an assertive 1px `#CBD5E1` container border.
- **Sticky Time Runners & Timeline Indicators:** Zero shadow; uses high-contrast layering with a 2px horizontal indicator in `#EA580C` and a persistent `#0F172A` pin badge.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a "Soft" corner language (Radius 1: `0.25rem` base, `0.5rem` cards, `0.75rem` floating modals). This geometry communicates athletic precision, structural solidity, and sport equipment engineering without feeling harsh or brutalist.

### Shape Geometry Directives
- **Buttons, Form Inputs & Filters:** 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radii for structured, crisp alignment.
- **Cards, Court Columns & Main Panels:** 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radii, preserving clean grid lines when nesting components.
- **Status Pills, Court Surface Badges & Role Switchers:** Full circular/pill radius (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual contrast against geometric booking grid blocks.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#EA580C`, text `#FFFFFF`, 0.25rem radius, font `Space Grotesk` Bold. Active state shifts to `#C2410C`. Focus rings feature an offset 2px `#EA580C` outline.
- **Secondary Action:** Pure white background, 1px solid `#CBD5E1` border, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions to `#F8FAFC` with a `#94A3B8` border.
- **Role Switcher (Mitglied / Extern / Platzwart):** Segmented control with `#F1F5F9` background track, 4px inner padding. The active selection uses a crisp white tile elevated by a 1px border and subtle ambient shadow, with role-specific micro-dots (Green for Member, Slate for External, Orange for Groundskeeper).

### Interactive Court Booking Grid
- **Slot Tile (Available):** Clean `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Hover reveals a preview pill showing price and quick "+ Reservieren" label with `#FFF7ED` background tint.
- **Slot Tile (Reserved / Match Scheduled):** `#F8FAFC` fill, left accent bar (4px solid) colored by surface type or booking class (e.g., Club League match, Training, Private). Contains player initials avatar chip and monospaced start/end times.
- **Slot Tile (Sperre / Platzwart):** Striped neutral warning pattern (45-degree micro-lines `#E2E8F0` over `#F8FAFC`) with bold label "GESPERRT" and reason tag (e.g., "Bewässerung / Abziehen").

### Badges, Status & Surface Chips
- **Surface Tags:** Compact pill components featuring uppercase `Space Grotesk` 11px text:
  - *Sand / Clay:* Rust `#C2410C` text, `#FFEDD5` background.
  - *Rasen / Grass:* Green `#15803D` text, `#DCFCE7` background.
  - *Hartplatz / Hard:* Cyan `#0369A1` text, `#E0F2FE` background.
  - *Halle / Indoor:* Amber `#B45309` text, `#FEF3C7` background.

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 8px padding vertical by 12px horizontal. Monospaced option for player member IDs and license codes.
- **Date Carousel / Mini-Calendar:** Horizontal day-picker with bold numeric dates (`Space Grotesk`), day of week labels in small caps slate, and active date highlighted by a solid `#EA580C` underbar or circular badge.

### Modals & Verification Sheets
- **Booking Drawer:** Slides in from the right edge on desktop or bottom sheet on mobile. Structured summary sections: court details, light condition / floodlight toggles (Flutlicht Aufpreis), guest fee calculations, player companion tagging with auto-complete, and final checkout lock-in timer.
